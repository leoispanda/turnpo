# Day 1 | Financial Management

> **Monday 7 September 2026 · 08:30–19:00**

## Overview

Day 1 establishes the language for investment and financing decisions. Move from incremental cash flow and value to liquidity, financing capacity, risk management and the effect of a decision on the wider enterprise.

## Key topics

- Incremental cash flow, opportunity cost, sunk cost and NPV.
- Discounting, required return, risk-return and financial markets.
- Debt, equity, liquidity, covenants and financing capacity.
- Diversification, derivatives, hedging and core versus non-core risk.
- Enterprise and portfolio effects: when a project creates concentration risk.

## Key takeaways

- A project decision uses the cash flows that change because of the project, not accounting profit or sunk cost alone.
- Positive NPV is necessary but not sufficient: the company must survive the cash timing and financing commitments.
- Hedging should protect a defined exposure and threshold; it should not replace business judgement.
- The final recommendation should state whether to invest, stage, delay or reject, and which assumption would change that decision.

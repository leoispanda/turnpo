# Course Syllabus | September 2026

> **7–11 September 2026 · 6 ECTS · MaastrichtMBA / EMBA**

## Scope

Corporate Finance & Accounting is a five-day module connecting financial management, compliance, sustainability reporting, financial accounting, enterprise risk management and management control.

- **Classroom:** 42 hours.
- **Self-study:** 126 hours.
- **Total workload:** 168 hours.

## Course week

| Day | Focus | Required reading route |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 · Monday 7 Sep | Financial Management | Berk; Stulz; Nocco & Stulz |
| Day 2 · Tuesday 8 Sep | Compliance, Sustainability Reporting & Leadership in Finance | COSO; Ewing; ING; De Micco et al.; KPMG; no separate reading for the guest talk |
| Day 3 · Wednesday 9 Sep | Financial Accounting, ERM & Governance | COSO Executive Summary; Grant Thornton; Deloitte; DSM; no separate reading for Accounting & Analysis or the police visit |
| Day 4 · Thursday 10 Sep | Management Accounting & Strategic Control | Tennessee Controls; Davila; Quinn & Thakor |
| Day 5 · Friday 11 Sep | Financial Management Integration & Presentation | Otten & Schweitzer |

## Assessment and preparation

The group presentation is worth **25%**. The confirmed Team 6 case is **BeFrank & Impact Investing**, presented from the employer/company perspective in the Friday final session; the maximum presentation time is 10 minutes and no fixed slide count is specified.

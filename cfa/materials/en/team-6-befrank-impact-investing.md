# Team 6 | BeFrank & Impact Investing

> **Assessment:** Group Presentation — 25%
> **Perspective:** Employer / company
> **Presentation:** Friday final session · maximum 10 minutes

## Assignment Guidance

Analyse the case from the employer/company perspective. BeFrank is a commercial pension provider and pension-solution partner, not a pension fund. Do not assume a BeFrank product, fee, investment option or impact metric that you have not verified.

## Assignment Questions

These are the confirmed decision questions:

1. Does impact investing make sense for the employees covered by the company’s pension arrangement?
2. If it does, should the employer ask BeFrank to incorporate more impact investing into its pension offering?
3. If BeFrank is not the most suitable partner or structure for the objective, what alternative pension arrangement or provider setup could the company consider?

## Decision Structure

```text
Employee relevance
        ↓
Employer rationale and trade-offs
        ↓
BeFrank suitability
        ↓
Alternative arrangement only if necessary
        ↓
Employer-level recommendation
```

## Relevant Research & Evidence

Build the recommendation from current, checkable evidence. Separate facts, inferences and assumptions.

- **Employees:** pension adequacy, risk, diversification, time horizon, employee preferences and understandable choice architecture.
- **Employer:** the employee outcome to improve or protect, cost, governance, communication and implementation feasibility.
- **BeFrank:** current investment options, impact definition and measurement, fees, reporting, administration and limits. Verify each claim with provider documentation or another reliable source.
- **Alternative:** compare another arrangement or provider only if BeFrank cannot adequately support the objective. Use the same criteria: employee outcome, investment proposition, transparency, employer feasibility and transition risk.

Do not treat a preference survey as proof of financial performance. Do not recommend a provider switch merely because an alternative sounds more ambitious.

## Team 6 Working Draft

The following is an optional team working structure, not a fixed slide count or a faculty requirement:

1. State the employee-centred decision and employer perspective.
2. Present evidence for employee relevance and the main trade-offs.
3. Explain the employer rationale and constraints.
4. Assess BeFrank using verified information.
5. Apply the conditional alternative-arrangement test.
6. Give the recommendation, evidence gaps, owner, review point and decision threshold.

## Presentation Requirements

- **Weight:** Group Presentation — 25%.
- **Timing:** Friday final session.
- **Maximum duration:** 10 minutes.
- **Submission:** submit the presentation materials through the course system afterwards.
- **Slides:** no fixed number has been specified; use only what makes the argument clear within ten minutes.

The conclusion should be an evidence-backed employer recommendation. State what could change it and what remains unverified.

# Day 3 | Financial Accounting, ERM & Governance

> **Wednesday 9 September 2026 · 08:30–19:00**

## Overview

Day 3 connects financial information, enterprise risk management and governance. Read statements with judgement, identify how risk reaches objectives and make ownership, challenge and escalation visible.

## Key topics

- Three financial statements, accruals, matching, estimates and accounting judgement.
- COSO ERM: governance and culture, strategy and objectives, performance, review and revision, and information and reporting.
- Risk appetite, risk indicators, non-financial risk and the Three Lines model.
- Governance in practice, including public value, accountability and disclosure.

## Key takeaways

- Accounting numbers are evidence, but estimates, definitions and timing determine how they should be interpreted.
- ERM begins with objectives and decisions, not with an unconnected list of risks.
- Governance is a system of ownership, challenge, information and follow-up; it is not a promise that risk will disappear.
- A useful KRI has a clear owner, threshold and response.

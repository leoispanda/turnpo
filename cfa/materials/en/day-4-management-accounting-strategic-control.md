# Day 4 | Management Accounting & Strategic Control

> **Thursday 10 September 2026 · 08:30–19:00**

## Overview

Day 4 asks how management accounting and control systems shape behaviour. Use Tennessee Controls, innovation research and purpose-driven organisation research to distinguish useful discipline from false precision, gaming and loss of dialogue.

## Key topics

- Strategic ranking, capital-expenditure decisions and the limits of a single score.
- False precision, gaming, interdependencies and the need for managerial dialogue.
- Simons’ four levers: belief systems, boundary systems, diagnostic controls and interactive controls.
- Control as information for innovation, and purpose as a guide within clear boundaries.

## Required readings

### 1. Simons & Geiger (1991; revised 2010)

- **Title:** *Tennessee Controls: The Strategic Ranking Problem*, HBS No. 191-083.
- **Focus:** how a quantitative ranking system can hide assumptions, encourage gaming, ignore project interdependencies and replace strategic discussion.
- **Links:** [Official HBS case page](https://store.hbr.org/product/tennessee-controls-the-strategic-ranking-problem/191083) · [Open the local case PDF](../2026-09/readings/Tennessee%20Controls.pdf)

### 2. Davila (2005)

- **Title:** “The Promise of Management Control Systems for Innovation and Strategic Change,” in *Controlling Strategy*, pp. 37–61.
- **Focus:** design controls as information and learning mechanisms that support innovation without turning uncertainty into a punishment system.
- **Links:** [Oxford Academic chapter record](https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780199283231.003.0003) · [Open the local study guide](../2026-09/readings/summaries/davila-2005-management-control-innovation-study-guide.md)

### 3. Quinn & Thakor (2018)

- **Title:** “Creating a Purpose-Driven Organization,” *Harvard Business Review*, 96(4), 78–85.
- **Focus:** how purpose, incentives, values and accountability interact when rules do not specify every decision.
- **Links:** [HBR article page](https://hbr.org/2018/07/creating-a-purpose-driven-organization) · [Open the local study guide](../2026-09/readings/summaries/quinn-thakor-2018-purpose-driven-organization-study-guide.md)

## Key takeaways

- A ranking score is a starting point for questions, not a substitute for judgement.
- Diagnostic measures should monitor execution; interactive controls should challenge assumptions and strategic uncertainty.
- Belief and boundary systems work together: purpose needs limits, evidence and accountability.

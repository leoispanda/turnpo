About Turnpo

What shaped you? What are you building?

Turnpo is a personal story and AI work profile platform. It helps people introduce themselves through turning points, defining stories, values, and the work they are building in the AI era.

It is not a social feed, a LinkedIn clone, or a private diary. It is a curated public profile that people can read and AI tools can understand from approved context.

Legal / Privacy / AI Disclaimer

Turnpo separates unpublished source material from published profile content. This static prototype uses demo/local owner mode and browser storage; it is not real backend authentication.

Please do not upload highly sensitive information such as passwords, government IDs, medical records, financial secrets, confidential company materials, or detailed private addresses.

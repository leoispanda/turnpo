About Turnpo

Turnpo is a personal story and AI work profile platform built around turning points.

It helps people introduce themselves beyond titles, resumes, job histories, and social feeds. A Turnpo profile brings together the moments that shaped a person, the stories they choose to keep, the values they want to show, and the work they are building in the AI era.

In the AI era, being understood is changing.

It is no longer enough to have a resume that only humans can scan. People are increasingly discovered, searched, summarized, compared, and introduced through AI tools. Turnpo helps create profiles that are clear for people and structured enough for AI to understand.

Turnpo is not a LinkedIn clone, not a daily feed, and not a private diary. It is a curated profile for understanding someone through the moments that changed their direction.

Human-readable. AI-readable.

Turnpo profiles are designed for two kinds of readers.

People can browse a clean, curated profile with timeline stories, defining moments, AI works, values, and links.

AI tools can read or receive a structured AI Profile based only on content the profile owner has approved for public sharing.

This makes a Turnpo profile easier to search, copy, summarize, and understand in AI-assisted contexts such as recruiting, collaboration, school applications, introductions, and personal branding.

The goal is simple: help people present themselves more effectively in the AI era, while keeping control over what becomes public.

Legal / Privacy / AI Disclaimer

How content works

Turnpo uses three content states.

Unpublished

Unpublished content is only visible to the profile owner in owner mode. It is not shown on the public profile, not included in the public AI Profile, and not intended for search engines or AI crawlers.

Published

Published content appears on the public Turnpo profile. It may be viewed, copied, indexed by search engines, or analyzed by AI systems and visitors.

Deleted

Deleted content is removed from Turnpo's active display and public outputs. If the content was previously published, Turnpo cannot fully control copies that may already exist outside the platform, such as screenshots, downloads, search caches, or external AI records.

Trust & privacy

You control what becomes public.

Unpublished content is not shown on your public profile or public AI-readable profile. AI drafts are never meant to be published without user approval.

Published content is designed to be shared. Please only publish information you are comfortable making public and potentially discoverable by people, search engines, and AI systems.

This prototype currently stores edited profile content in browser storage. Do not use it to store sensitive or confidential information.

Please do not upload highly sensitive information such as passwords, government IDs, medical records, financial secrets, confidential company materials, private addresses, or information you do not have the right to share.

AI assistance

Turnpo AI assistance is coming soon.

In a real production version, AI processing must happen server-side, with proper authentication, user consent, and data protection. AI output should remain a draft until the profile owner reviews and approves it.

Turnpo should help shape a story, not define a person without their approval.

Deletion notice

If you delete content, Turnpo will remove it from the platform's active public display and public outputs. However, if that content was previously published, others may already have copied, cached, indexed, downloaded, screenshotted, or analyzed it outside Turnpo.

Turnpo cannot fully control copies made outside the platform.

For this reason, please publish thoughtfully.

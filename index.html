02

03

Legal / Privacy / AI Disclaimer

No fake backend security claims.

Turnpo separates unpublished source material from published profile content. This static prototype uses demo/local owner mode and localStorage; it is not real backend authentication.

Please do not upload highly sensitive information such as passwords, government IDs, medical records, financial secrets, confidential company materials, or detailed private addresses.

Turnpo AI assistance is coming soon. AI calls must be server-side in a real backend, and AI output must remain draft-only until user approval.

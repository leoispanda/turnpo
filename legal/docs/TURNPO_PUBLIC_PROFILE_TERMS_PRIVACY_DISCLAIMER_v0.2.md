## 1. Plain-Language Summary

Turnpo helps people organize their life, work, learning, projects, turning points, and personal stories into a structured public profile.

Please treat anything you add to Turnpo as potentially public.

When you register for Turnpo, create a profile, upload content, import text, use AI assistance, or publish a story, you should assume that the information may be seen by anyone, including strangers, employers, recruiters, colleagues, clients, schools, family members, search engines, AI systems, web crawlers, and third-party services.

## 2. What Turnpo Is

Turnpo is a public profile and personal story platform.

It is intended to help users present selected parts of their life, work, learning, projects, achievements, reflections, and turning points in a structured format that can be understood by both humans and AI systems.

Turnpo may allow users to create or display:

- A public profile
- A personal timeline
- Life stories or turning points
- Work and project highlights
- AI-readable profile summaries
- Imported stories from CVs, notes, LinkedIn-style posts, personal writing, or other source text
- Images, links, tags, summaries, reflections, and other profile materials
- AI-assisted drafts or structured outputs based on user-provided materials

Turnpo is not designed as a private diary, confidential storage system, legal archive, medical record system, encrypted vault, employment verification system, or secure document repository.

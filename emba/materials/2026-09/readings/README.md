## How to use this checklist

- **Public**: can be downloaded from the linked publisher, author, company, or university source.
- **Library / Canvas / purchase**: obtain through Maastricht University Library, Canvas, or the publisher; no attempt should be made to bypass access controls.
- **Web reading**: the syllabus assigns an online article or webpage rather than a downloadable course PDF.

# Corporate Finance and Accounting - September 2026

## Course week

7-11 September 2026. This 6-ECTS module connects financial management, financial accounting and analysis, management accounting and control, enterprise risk management, compliance, and sustainability reporting.

## Course outcome

The practical goal is financial leadership: make investment, financing and risk decisions using sound financial information, while balancing performance, governance, compliance and long-term stakeholder value.

## Schedule

- Monday: Financial Management
- Tuesday: Compliance, Sustainability Reporting, Leadership in Finance guest talk
- Wednesday: Financial Accounting and Analysis, Enterprise Risk Management, Limburg Regional Police visit
- Thursday: Management Accounting and Control
- Friday: Financial Management and MBA graduation

## Assessment

- Group presentation: 25%, in class on 11 September 2026

- Individual-case grading: content/application 75%, critical reflection (including AI-use reflection) 15%, form and APA references 10%

# Team Building - Diana Mingo Berdun

## 1. One-Line Summary

The session uses low-stakes group exercises to move a new MBA cohort from remembering names toward trust, non-verbal coordination, personal disclosure, and a written intention for the future.

## 2. Source And Privacy Rule

This ten-page slide deck contains a complete cohort grouping list. Classmate names are intentionally not reproduced in this searchable Markdown. Open the protected PDF when the original grouping evidence is needed.

## 3. Session Sequence

The agenda is introduction, bonding, team-building, and fun. The activities progress through increasing interpersonal depth:

1. Name Juggling: learn and recall names.
2. Birthday Timeline: coordinate by month and day without speaking, writing, or showing identification.
3. Musical Questions: rotate partners, introduce yourself, answer prompts, and exchange prompt papers.
4. M&M exercise: answer a personal question linked to a selected colour over repeated group turns.
5. Future-self card: choose an image representing the impact you want to have after the MBA and write a message to your future self.
6. Wrap-up: thoughts, feedback, and a cohort selfie.

## 4. Learning Meaning

The birthday exercise is a small experiment in communication under constraint. It asks what helped a group coordinate when normal speech was removed. The answer is not a single technique; it is the group's ability to observe, signal, adapt, and check shared understanding.

The later exercises add personal exchange. Their purpose is less about completing a game and more about creating enough familiarity for future learning groups, feedback, and disagreement.

## 5. Future-Self Prompt

The final individual task asks:

- What impact do I want to have on others after completing the MBA?
- How do I want to experience the programme?
- What do I want to have achieved by the end?
- What should my future self remember from today's expectations?

This card is potentially useful evidence for a later before-and-after review. It should be revisited at mid-term and before graduation.

## 6. Connection To Leo's Reflection

The session provides context for psychological safety, inclusion, listening, and cohort learning, but the slides do not contain Leo's own conclusions. A useful follow-up is to recover the image Leo selected and the exact message written to his future self.

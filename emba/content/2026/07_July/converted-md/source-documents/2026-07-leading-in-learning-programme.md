## 5. Session Logic

The official programme moves through five learning layers:

1. Orientation: welcome, need-to-knows, team building.
2. Personal leadership: Leadership Development Trajectory and ownership of self-development.
3. Learning capacity: brain, mind, cognitive schemas, learning, unlearning, relearning.
4. Organizational learning: how leaders foster learning for themselves, teams, and organizations.
5. Communication and inclusion: visualisation, prototyping, storytelling, culture, diversity, and collaboration.

## 7. Travel And Arrival Notes

The PDF warns that car navigation can be tricky around Tapijn because the campus sits inside a walking area. It also notes that Tapijn Kazerne is in a central parking zone with higher rates.

Train option:

- travel to Maastricht Central Station;
- walk about 20 minutes;
- or take a bus to Tongersestraat and walk about 6 minutes from there.

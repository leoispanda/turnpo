# MBA Need To Knows - Jesca Rijpkema

## 1. One-Line Summary

This deck is the operational handbook for starting the MBA: finish access setup, prepare in Canvas, understand registration and assessment rules, plan LDT and the Business Consulting Project, and know where to ask for help.

## 2. Source And Privacy Rule

The protected 21-page PDF is the administrative source. One slide contains the July 2026 participant roster and profile-picture groups; those classmate names are intentionally not reproduced in searchable Markdown.

Dates, contacts, fees, rules, and platform instructions can change. Verify the current syllabus, Canvas, Teams, and official regulations before acting.

## 3. Immediate Next Steps

1. Complete the onboarding guideline and obtain access to all required applications and websites.
2. Open Canvas and prepare for the first class through the syllabus and assigned materials.
3. Record the track dates shown in the deck: online introduction on 2026-09-06 and on-campus core-module start on 2026-09-07.

The on-campus track is automatically enrolled in on-campus modules. Both tracks receive elective-week invitations and registration through Teams and Qualtrics.

## 4. Assignments And Examinations

The deck lists possible assessment forms:

- essay;
- presentation;
- participation;
- reflection;
- individual or team assessment;
- numeric grading from 0 to 10 or Pass/Fail.

Submissions are made digitally through Canvas in PDF format. Due dates must be checked in each course syllabus. The source gives a typical six-week deadline after an educational week and assessment during a six-week online course.

It states that one re-examination or resit is available per course, with deadlines set by teaching faculty, and that a resit removes eligibility for Cum Laude. It also warns that plagiarism and self-plagiarism are checked. Copyrighted articles should not be redistributed.

Evidence boundary: the official Education and Examination Regulations and current course syllabus outrank this introductory slide summary.

## 5. Long-Running Programme Components

### Business Consulting Project

- Begins in the second half of the MBA journey, in June or December according to the source.
- Applies MBA learning to a problem in the participant's own organization.
- Is discussed with the BCP coordinator and guided by a supervisor.
- Uses BCP webinars and a formal timeline.

### Leadership Development Trajectory

- Starts according to intake size and programme timing, no later than six months after the start according to the deck.
- Develops self-awareness and skills.
- Includes individual coaching, group sessions, and buddy coaching.

## 6. Programme Contacts

The source separates responsibility across:

- on-campus modules and elective weeks;
- online modules and elective weeks;
- Leading in Learning, graduation, LDT, BCP, and elective coordination.

Open the original or current programme channels for names and current contact details.

## 7. Core Modules And Electives

The on-campus core modules shown are:

- Corporate Finance and Accounting;
- Sustaining Competitive Advantage;
- Understanding Organisations in Times of Change;
- Entrepreneurship and New Business Development;
- International Environment of Business.

The three elective themes are Sustainable Innovation, Digital Business, and Responsible Leadership. Each theme contains two Maastricht-based weeks and one international-perspectives week abroad in the source map.

## 8. Platforms And Annual Administration

The deck identifies:

- eduroam for university Wi-Fi;
- Maastricht University student email;
- Canvas for course information;
- the university library for academic articles;
- Studielink for annual registration updates;
- LinkedIn and Instagram for social connection.

UMIO is described as the executive-education branch of Maastricht University School of Business and Economics and the institutional home of MaastrichtMBA.

## 9. Leo Operating Checklist

- Confirm access to email, Canvas, Teams, library, and eduroam.
- Add every assessment deadline from the syllabus, not from memory.
- Keep submitted work and source notes separated to reduce self-plagiarism risk.
- Decide when to begin BCP topic discovery rather than waiting for the formal start.
- Link each elective choice to a concrete company-building or leadership question.
- Recheck annual Studielink registration.

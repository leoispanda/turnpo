# Alumni Forum - Jeroen Duijsinx

## 1. One-Line Summary

The alumni forum positions the MBA network as a long-term learning and professional relationship, then invites recent alumni to explain the lived journey, impact, network effects, and advice for new students.

## 2. Alumni Network Layers

The deck lists several ways to remain connected:

- MBA Graduation Day;
- MaastrichtMBA Alumni Association events and networking;
- Maastricht University and SBE alumni events;
- UMIO Inspiration Day;
- Breakfast Booster sessions;
- online webinars;
- wider lifelong learning through UMIO.

## 3. MaastrichtMBA Alumni Association

The association is described as student-created and founded at the end of 2024. Its purpose is to preserve the value of professional networks and lifelong learning through events and networking opportunities.

The source lists a membership fee of EUR 40 per year. Treat the fee and membership process as source-time information and verify the current terms before joining.

## 4. Alumni Speakers

The forum introduces two recent alumni from the online and on-campus tracks, with backgrounds spanning gene therapy, analytics, digitalization, and business administration.

Their roles and employers are historical context from the July 2026 deck and may change. Open the original source for the exact biographies.

## 5. MBA Journey Questions

The discussion is organized around:

1. Why the alumni opted into the MBA.
2. What the MBA experience was actually like.
3. What professional impact followed.
4. How the network created value.
5. What advice they would give new students.
6. Remaining participant questions.

## 6. Suggested Leo Follow-Up

The slides do not record the alumni's spoken answers. To make this session useful later, Leo can add what he remembers under three headings:

- What surprised me about the real workload or experience?
- What professional impact seemed credible and why?
- Which networking behaviour should I begin now rather than after graduation?

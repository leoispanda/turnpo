# IMG 6900 - Lead in Learning

## Source

- Original image: `/emba/materials/2026-07/handwritten-notes/images/IMG_6900.jpeg`
- Overall Markdown: `../2026-07-01-leadership-learning-handwritten-notes.md`

## Clean Markdown Text

This page appears to capture the opening Lead in Learning setup for the July EMBA introduction. The notes point to onboarding, learning groups, coaching groups, and the expectation that leadership questions should come from real personal struggle, real interest, and daily work situations.

Key points:

- Intro programme and practical onboarding context.
- Learning groups and coaching groups.
- Leadership questions should come from lived work tension, not abstract curiosity only.
- Coaching starts with noticing automatic patterns and assumptions.
- Shifting perspective is part of the learning task.
- Leadership is connected to enabling others to think differently.

## Interpretation

The practical shift is from collecting course content to building a reflective operating system. The EMBA is framed as a place to bring real leadership friction, test assumptions, and return to work with a changed way of seeing.

## Personal Application

- Bring one live leadership question to each module.
- Before seeking an answer, ask what assumption is shaping the question.
- Use coaching conversations to reveal patterns, not only to receive advice.

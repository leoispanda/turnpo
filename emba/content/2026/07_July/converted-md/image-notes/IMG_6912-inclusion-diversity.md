# IMG 6912 - Inclusion And Diversity

## Clean Markdown Text

This page records the Inclusion and Diversity session from 2026-07-03.

Key points:

- Where you are from and where you currently belong both matter.
- Communication carries culture.
- The first feeling may be autopilot.
- The second feeling may be focus.
- The Culture Map helps make differences visible.
- Inclusion is practiced through how people are heard, interpreted, and invited into the conversation.

## Interpretation

Inclusion is not only a value statement. It becomes real through listening, belonging, communication norms, and awareness of autopilot reactions.

## Personal Application

- Notice the first automatic reaction in cross-cultural situations.
- Ask what belonging looks like for the other person.
- Use culture mapping before assuming intent.

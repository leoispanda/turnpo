# IMG 6906 - Organizational Learning And Change

## Source

- Original image: `/emba/materials/2026-07/handwritten-notes/images/IMG_6906.jpeg`
- Overall Markdown: `../2026-07-01-leadership-learning-handwritten-notes.md`

## Clean Markdown Text

This page connects learning with change, courage, and movement beyond the comfort zone.

Key points:

- Learning is a process, not just a result.
- Work benefits from repeated learning loops.
- Change requires courage.
- The leader should not simply make a comfortable room.
- Leadership can help people think differently and act differently.

## Interpretation

The page suggests that organizational learning is not a training event. It is the repeated conversion of experience into changed behavior.

## Personal Application

- After a project or meeting, name one behavior to change.
- Repeat the behavior until it becomes usable under pressure.
- Avoid equating comfort with learning.

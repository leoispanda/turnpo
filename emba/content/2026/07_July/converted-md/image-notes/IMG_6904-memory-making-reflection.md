## Interpretation

The page treats reflection as a way to convert experience into learning. Events alone are points. Reflection connects them into patterns and meaning.

## Personal Application

- Schedule a weekly reflection appointment.
- After important events, write what happened, what it may mean, and what else could be true.
- Treat blind spots as information rather than embarrassment.

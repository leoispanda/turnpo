# IMG 6908 - 70/20/10 And Knowledge Creation

## Source

- Original image: `/emba/materials/2026-07/handwritten-notes/images/IMG_6908.jpeg`
- Overall Markdown: `../2026-07-01-leadership-learning-handwritten-notes.md`

## Clean Markdown Text

This page records the organizational learning frame.

Key points:

- 70/20/10 learning: most learning happens on the job, then through informal learning, then through formal learning.
- On-the-job learning needs to be named and made visible.
- Nonaka and Takeuchi are connected to knowledge creation.
- Observe, explain, practice, and combine.
- Practice continues until behavior becomes second nature.

## Interpretation

The main insight is that organizations do not learn just because individuals experience something. Learning has to be observed, explained, practiced, combined, and stored in shared language or routines.

## Personal Application

- Turn one useful lesson into a team routine or checklist.
- Use after-action reviews to make work learning visible.
- Capture repeated practice, not only final conclusions.

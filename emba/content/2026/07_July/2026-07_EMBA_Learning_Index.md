## 7. Work Applications

- Before meetings: write the intended outcome and likely assumptions.
- During meetings: listen longer, summarize before responding, and ask one question that helps others think differently.
- After meetings: capture what happened, what I made it mean, and what else could be true.
- With AI: use it to summarize, roleplay, pressure-test, and find blind spots, while keeping human judgment in the lead.
- For teams: turn useful insight into shared language, meeting rhythm, checklist, or story.

Business Challenge FD Mediagroep – Executive MBA Maastricht University 
 
Strategic context 
FD Mediagroep’s purpose is to help the leaders of today and tomorrow make better 
decisions and drive progress. Its ESG strategy supports this purpose through four areas 
closely connected to the core business: independent and high-quality journalism, 
responsible use of data and information, being a good employer, and reducing its 
carbon footprint. 
 
Having strengthened the foundations across these priorities, the next challenge is to 
determine how responsible business can become a more integral part of FDMG’s long-
term strategy and value creation. 
 
This is increasingly relevant as developments around AI, trust in information, digital 
rights, talent and environmental impact intersect with fundamental business questions 
around innovation, growth, risk and resilience. The strategic question is therefore not 
simply how FDMG can “do more ESG”, but which ESG issues will matter most to its 
future success – and how they should shape strategic choices towards 2030. 
 
Challenge formulation 
How can FD Mediagroep integrate responsible business into its long-term strategy 
to strengthen resilience and create sustainable societal and commercial value 
towards 2030? 
 
Possible research areas 
• Which ESG issues will be most strategically relevant to FDMG towards 2030? 
• Where does responsible business primarily protect value, and where can it 
create new value? 
• What should “responsible growth” mean for FDMG in light of its purpose? 
• How should ESG influence strategic choices around AI, products, talent, 
partnerships, acquisitions and growth? 
• Where should FDMG lead, and where is meeting stakeholder expectations 
sufficient? 
 
Potential outcome 
A prioritised strategic perspective towards 2030, identifying the ESG issues most 
important to FDMG’s future and distinguishing where they contribute to licence to 
operate, risk mitigation, strategic differentiation or value creation. This should translate 
into a limited number of strategic priorities and choices that can help FDMG create 
long-term societal and commercial value while delivering on its purpose.

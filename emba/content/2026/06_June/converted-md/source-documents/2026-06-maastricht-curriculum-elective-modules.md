# MaastrichtMBA Curriculum Elective Modules

## 1. One-Line Summary

This is the elective strategy source: it explains what Digital Business, Responsible Leadership, and Sustainable Innovation actually mean at module level.

## 2. Elective Theme Map

| Theme | Module 1 | Module 2 | Module 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Digital Business | Digital Management | Digital Strategy | Perspectives on Digital Business |
| Responsible Leadership | Leading Strategic Change | Personal Leadership | Perspectives on Leadership |
| Sustainable Innovation | Sustainability and Business Ethics | Future-Proof Business Models | Perspectives on Sustainability |

## 3. Digital Business

Digital Business is the strongest fit for AI, product building, data, smart services, platform thinking, and digital organization design.

Core ideas:

- analytics as a management capability;
- data management as the base layer for useful analytics;
- digital case studies across business functions;
- digital maturity and digital organization design;
- digital twins, digital selves, smart services, AI, big data, GDPR, and service prototyping.

Leo-specific interpretation:

- MapKAI, Turnpo, DishKAI, AI workflows, and product strategy all fit this theme.
- Digital Strategy seems especially relevant because it connects data, algorithms, user perspective, business model design, and societal risk.

## 4. Responsible Leadership

Responsible Leadership is the strongest fit for founder growth, coaching, team learning, psychological safety, and leading change.

Core ideas:

- strategic change differs from normal project planning;
- dialogue matters as a distinct form of exchange;
- personal preferences influence process flow;
- conflicting interests need constructive handling;
- modern decision-making needs transparency and respect for emancipated individuals;
- leadership rests on self-knowledge, reflection, values, courage, embodiment, storytelling, and honest connection.

Leo-specific interpretation:

- This theme connects directly to July's Leading in Learning notes.
- It is likely the most personal-growth-heavy elective path.

## 5. Sustainable Innovation

Sustainable Innovation is the strongest fit for impact, SDGs, circular economy, business ethics, and future-proof business model design.

Core ideas:

- wicked problems, planetary boundaries, social justice, and sustainability worldviews;
- CSR, shared value, social entrepreneurship, circular economy, and business ethics;
- stakeholder engagement and SDG-driven strategy;
- business model innovation and service design;
- design thinking, speculative futures, imagineering, and innovation process design.

Leo-specific interpretation:

- This theme is useful if the EMBA should strengthen impact language and business model redesign.
- It may be less immediately connected to current AI/product work than Digital Business, but more connected to long-term public value and sustainability.

## 6. Provisional Elective Thesis

Best current thesis:

- Primary path: two Digital Business weeks plus one Responsible Leadership week.
- Personal-growth path: two Responsible Leadership weeks plus one Digital Business week.
- Impact path: two Sustainable Innovation weeks plus one Digital Business week.

Do not finalize this in June. Use July and the first core module to test which theme creates the most useful personal and company-building leverage.

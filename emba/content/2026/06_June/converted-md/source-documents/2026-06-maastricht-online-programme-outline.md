# MaastrichtMBA Online Programme Outline 2026-2027

## 1. One-Line Summary

This document is not the active track for Leo, but it is useful comparison material for understanding how the online path differs from the on-campus EMBA.

## 2. Source Role

Classify this file under June 2026 as track-selection and programme-planning context. It should not override the on-campus outline.

Use it for:

- comparing online and on-campus workload design;
- confirming shared elective rules;
- understanding the online course rhythm;
- avoiding confusion when programme communications mention online-track elements.

## 3. Online Track Structure

The online track consists of:

- face-to-face induction programme, Leading in Learning;
- ten online courses;
- three face-to-face elective weeks;
- Leadership Development Trajectory;
- Business Research Methods;
- Business Consulting Project.

Online courses are described as six-week journeys, while elective weeks remain five-day face-to-face pressure-cooker programmes.

## 4. Main Difference From On-Campus Track

| Dimension | Online Track | On-Campus Track |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core curriculum | Ten online courses | Five face-to-face core modules |
| Core rhythm | Six-week online journeys | Five-day in-person module weeks |
| Electives | Three face-to-face elective weeks | Three face-to-face elective weeks |
| Induction | Leading in Learning | Leading in Learning |
| Best use in Leo's system | Comparison only | Active study path |

## 5. Shared Elective Rules

The online outline uses the same broad elective logic:

- three elective weeks out of nine;
- at least one in or around Maastricht;
- at least one abroad;
- second abroad week adds EUR 1,000;
- two abroad weeks cannot be from the same theme;
- at least two weeks from one theme can support diploma supplement recognition.

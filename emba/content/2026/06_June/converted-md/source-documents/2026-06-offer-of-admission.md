The offer describes the on-campus programme as:

This structure is the contractual high-level view. The current programme outline should be used for detailed week-by-week planning.

- Official enrolment follows receipt of the signed acceptance.
- The document states that the offer remains valid for acceptance until 2027-08-31.

For cancellation, continuation, instalment timing, or any financial decision, verify both this offer and the separate fee-policy PDF.

# MaastrichtMBA On-Campus Programme Outline 2026-2027

## 1. One-Line Summary

This is the active programme-path document for the on-campus EMBA: it defines the calendar architecture, core module rhythm, elective structure, and elective rules.

## 3. Programme Structure

The on-campus student participates in:

- face-to-face induction programme, Leading in Learning;
- five face-to-face core modules;
- three face-to-face elective weeks;
- Leadership Development Trajectory;
- Business Research Methods;
- Business Consulting Project.

The outline notes that Leadership Development Trajectory and Business Consulting Project are student-led or fluid in timing, so they are not fully visible in the dated calendar grid.

## 6. Elective Rules

The elective system has three themes:

- Digital Business;
- Responsible Leadership;
- Sustainable Innovation.

Each theme offers:

- two weeks in or around Maastricht;
- one week abroad.

Rules:

- choose three out of nine elective weeks;
- at least one elective week must be in or around Maastricht;
- at least one elective week must be abroad;
- a second abroad week increases tuition by EUR 1,000;
- two abroad weeks cannot focus on the same theme;
- for diploma supplement recognition of a theme, complete at least two weeks from that theme.

## 7. Leo-Specific Planning Implication

This outline supports a provisional elective thesis:

- Digital Business is the strongest fit for AI, product, data, and company-building work.
- Responsible Leadership is the strongest fit for founder development, coaching, and organization-building.
- Sustainable Innovation is useful if the EMBA becomes a vehicle for impact or future-proof business model design.

# Nyenrode Impact MBA Executive Brochure

## 1. One-Line Summary

This brochure is not the active programme path, but it is useful comparison material for the meaning of impact, sustainability, personal leadership, and career development.

## 2. Source Role

Classify this file under June 2026 as selection-context material. Maastricht is the enrolled path; Nyenrode remains a comparison lens.

Use it to answer:

- why the Maastricht path should still define "impact" clearly;
- how another Dutch MBA frames leadership and sustainability;
- what career/community language Leo may want to borrow or reject.

## 3. Programme Positioning

Nyenrode positions the Impact MBA Executive around:

- purpose-driven leadership;
- sustainability and digitalization;
- global perspective;
- personal growth;
- a 24-month part-time format;
- lifelong community and alumni network.

## 4. Curriculum Map

The brochure groups the curriculum around:

- Fundamentals of Sustainable Business;
- Fundamentals of Management;
- Business in Motion;
- Managing the Organization;
- Global Immersion Program;
- Meet the CEO sessions;
- Personal Leadership Development Journey;
- Final Thesis.

Course themes include sustainable development, circularity, global economic dynamics, responsible marketing, data-driven decision making, strategic management, managerial finance, organizational behavior, entrepreneurship, innovation and digitalization, applied research methods, operations, organizational change, and sustainable finance.

## 5. Practical Format And Cost Notes

The brochure describes:

- classes roughly once a month;
- Wednesday to Friday full days plus Saturday morning blocks;
- tuition listed as EUR 49,000;
- separate pre-MBA course fees;
- LTP-test fee;
- no admissions fee.

These are not Maastricht obligations. They are comparison facts only.

## 7. Leo-Specific Interpretation

The brochure is most useful as a mirror:

- Does the Maastricht EMBA still need an explicit impact thesis?
- How should "impact" connect to Turnpo, MapKAI, AI, and leadership?
- Which parts of the MBA should become career capital, and which should become company-building capital?
- How can personal leadership work avoid becoming abstract self-development and instead become visible behavior?

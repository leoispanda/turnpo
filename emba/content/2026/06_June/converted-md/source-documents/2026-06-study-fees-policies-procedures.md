# MaastrichtMBA Study Fees, Policies And Procedures

## 1. One-Line Summary

This is the financial-risk document for the EMBA: fees, instalments, payment term, cancellation fee, premature termination rule, and continuation fee.

## 2. Core Financial Facts

| Item | Amount Or Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Application fee | EUR 200 |
| Executive MBA On-Campus track | EUR 34,500 |
| Executive MBA Online track | EUR 29,500 |
| On-campus instalments | Six instalments of EUR 5,750 |
| Payment term | 30 days per invoice |
| Late cancellation fee | EUR 500 after the relevant deadline |
| Premature termination | 25% of all remaining instalments |
| Maximum study duration | Four years |
| Continuation fee beyond maximum duration | EUR 3,200 per extra year, if approved |

## 3. September/November Start Rhythm

For an on-campus September/November start, the six invoice windows are:

1. September
2. January
3. May
4. September
5. January
6. May

The action is to convert these into calendar reminders with a 30-day payment window.

## 4. What The Fees Cover

The tuition covers:

- programme components;
- access to physical and virtual university learning facilities;
- catering during educational weeks, such as lunches, coffee breaks, and welcome dinners.

The tuition does not cover:

- travel;
- accommodation;
- e-books;
- other study materials.

## 5. Cancellation And Participation Risk

The programme automatically enrolls students in core curriculum units. The student must review the personal schedule and deregister when needed.

Each educational unit can have its own cancellation deadline:

- online course;
- on-campus week;
- elective week;
- international week.

The important behavior is to treat every invitation or confirmation from the MBA office as a financial document, not only a calendar message.

## 6. Work Application

- Build a payment and cancellation calendar.
- Add reminders before each invoice month.
- Track cancellation deadlines separately from travel deadlines.
- Before registering for electives, check travel and accommodation costs outside tuition.
- Keep this policy linked from the June preparation index.

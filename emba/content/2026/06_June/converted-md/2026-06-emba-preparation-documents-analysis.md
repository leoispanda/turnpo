## 5. Administrative And Financial Readiness

Key financial points from the Maastricht study-fee policy:

- Application fee: EUR 200.
- Executive MBA On-Campus track: EUR 34,500.
- Executive MBA Online track: EUR 29,500.
- On-campus payment is spread across six instalments of EUR 5,750 over two years.
- For a September/November start rhythm, invoice months are September, January, May, September, January, May.
- Payment term is 30 days.
- Late cancellation after the relevant deadline can trigger a non-refundable EUR 500 fee.
- Premature termination can trigger a charge of 25% of all remaining instalments.
- Study duration can be extended only with approval, with an additional annual continuation fee of EUR 3,200 beyond the maximum duration.

Preparation implication:

- Put the six payment windows into the calendar now.
- Treat every module registration and cancellation deadline as financially meaningful.
- Keep the offer letter, fee policy, and enrolment certificate together.

## 8. Elective Strategy

The elective structure has three themes:

- Digital Business.
- Responsible Leadership.
- Sustainable Innovation.

Each theme has three weeks:

- Two weeks in or around Maastricht.
- One international/abroad week.

Rules from the programme outline:

- Choose three elective weeks out of nine.
- At least one elective week must be completed in or around Maastricht.
- At least one elective week must be completed abroad.
- A second abroad week increases tuition by EUR 1,000.
- Two abroad weeks cannot focus on the same elective theme.
- For diploma supplement recognition of an elective theme, complete at least two weeks from that theme.

Best-fit interpretation for Leo:

- Digital Business is highly aligned with AI, Turnpo, MapKAI, product building, data, smart services, and digital strategy.
- Responsible Leadership is highly aligned with founder development, coaching, organizational learning, and cross-cultural leadership.
- Sustainable Innovation is useful if the EMBA is meant to strengthen impact, business-model redesign, and social/environmental strategy.

Provisional elective thesis:

- Primary path: two Digital Business weeks plus one Responsible Leadership abroad week.
- Alternative path: two Responsible Leadership weeks plus one Digital Business abroad week.
- Impact-heavy path: two Sustainable Innovation weeks plus one Digital Business week.

Do not finalize too early. Use July and the first core module to test which theme produces the most practical growth.

## 9. Strategic-Thinking Reading

The MIT Sloan article is a good pre-EMBA mental model. Its core argument is that managers promoted for execution can become underprepared for senior strategic roles if they do not deliberately rebuild thinking time.

Useful ideas to bring into the EMBA:

- Strategic thinking is not the same as fast problem solving.
- Strategy often starts with a specific use case, then tests how assumptions change the answer.
- Good strategy work looks for boundaries: where the company can compete and where it should not.
- Exhaustiveness is less important than depth.
- Delegation is not only an execution tool. It creates room for thinking.
- Senior leaders need protected thinking routines, not only action routines.

Personal application:

- Before each module, write one real strategic question from work.
- Use one customer, user, or stakeholder case as the starting point.
- Ask which assumptions would change the conclusion.
- After each module, define one decision boundary more clearly than before.

# EMBA Monthly Learning Index - June 2026

## 1. Month Overview

June 2026 should be treated as the EMBA preparation stage. The evidence from the documents says the path has moved beyond "application" and into "accepted, enrolled, and preparing to start."

The practical meaning of this month:

- Admission has been granted for the Executive Master of Business Administration, on-campus, part-time track.
- Enrolment is documented for academic year 2026/27.
- The first required dates are already close: the official introduction and Leading in Learning programme on 2026-07-01 to 2026-07-03.
- The working focus is now readiness: access, calendar, payment schedule, source document archive, and study strategy.

## 1A. Searchable Summary

June 2026 is best retrieved for questions about admission, enrolment proof, onboarding setup, Canvas/Teams access, tuition and cancellation policies, programme calendar planning, elective strategy, and the shift from application mode into an operating EMBA learning system.

## 2. Main Courses And Sessions

June is not yet a teaching month. It prepares for:

- Leading in Learning, starting 2026-07-01.

## 6. Personal Reflections

June 2026 is the month where the EMBA becomes operational. The important personal move is to avoid treating this as a paperwork folder. It is the start of a two-year learning architecture.

The preparation question is: what do I want this EMBA to change in how I think, lead, build, and make decisions?

## 7. Work Applications

- Block calendar time for every confirmed programme week.
- Create a one-page EMBA operating checklist for access, travel, lodging, reading, reflection, and post-module work application.
- Pick an elective hypothesis early, then update it after the first modules.
- Use each course to generate one practical experiment in Turnpo, MapKAI, DishKAI, or current leadership work.

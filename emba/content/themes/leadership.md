## Current Synthesis

Leadership is emerging as a practice of designing better thinking conditions. June frames the EMBA as a serious operating system, not only a degree. July turns that operating system inward and interpersonal: notice autopilot, listen longer, coach before advising, make space for disagreement, and turn learning into shared routines.

## Key Concepts

- Leader as builder of better thinking conditions.
- Listening before advising.
- Psychological safety with accountability.
- Coaching as helping the other person reframe their own problem.
- Inclusion as a behavioral practice, not a slogan.
- Organizational learning as shared language, rhythm, checklist, or story.

## Running Questions

- How can leadership create better thinking conditions?
- What does it mean to listen before advising?
- How can psychological safety and accountability exist together?
- Where am I still leading from autopilot?
- Which leadership insight should become a repeated team routine?

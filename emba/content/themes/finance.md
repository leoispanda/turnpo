## Current Synthesis

The finance theme currently starts with programme economics rather than corporate finance. June's preparation documents define tuition, instalment rhythm, cancellation exposure, payment timing, and continuation-fee risk. The immediate task is not analysis for class; it is making the EMBA financially operational.

## Key Concepts

- Tuition commitment and instalment calendar.
- Payment term and arrears risk.
- Cancellation deadlines and non-refundable fee exposure.
- Premature termination liability.
- Continuation fee if the study duration extends beyond approved limits.

## Running Questions

- Are all fee windows and 30-day payment reminders in the calendar?
- Which cancellation or module-registration deadlines create real financial exposure?
- Should EMBA costs be tracked as education investment, founder development, or both?

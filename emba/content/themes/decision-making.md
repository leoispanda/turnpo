## Current Synthesis

Decision-making appears early in two forms. June is practical and evidence-based: resolve the September date mismatch, calendar payments, verify source documents, and avoid choosing electives too late. July is reflective and behavioral: notice assumptions, separate facts from interpretation, and listen before deciding.

## Key Concepts

- Evidence chain before action.
- Source verification for administrative and financial decisions.
- Assumptions and blind spots.
- Perspective shifting.
- Date mismatch and open-check discipline.
- Elective decisions as strategic choices.

## Running Questions

- Which assumptions am I standing on?
- What else could be true?
- Which decision needs source verification rather than memory?
- What is the smallest experiment that would improve this decision?
- Which elective choice would I regret postponing?
